Maximum Stability with Just 4 Implants

The All-on-4 system is an innovative technique that supports an entire arch of replacement teeth using only four anchor points. By placing the implants at strategic angles, we maximize the use of your existing jawbone, providing a solid foundation for life.

4 Premium Titanium Implants

High-strength artificial roots integrated into your jawbone, designed to last a lifetime.

4 Multi-Unit Abutments

The connectors that guarantee maximum security between the implant and your new prosthesis.
